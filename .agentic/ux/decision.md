# Statements screen: variant review

Scored 1-5 at 1440px and 390px widths.

| Variant | Main job speed | Clarity | Accessibility | Tone | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: dense table | 3 (scan a table, small targets on mobile) | 4 | 3 (6 columns overflow at 390px) | 4 | 14 |
| B: latest-first cards | 5 (one large "Open statement" button) | 5 | 5 (44px targets, single column, status in words) | 5 | **20** |
| C: to-do queue | 3 (checkboxes add a step nobody needs) | 3 | 4 | 3 (feels like a task app) | 13 |

**Pick: B**, because investors come once a quarter on a phone to open one document. We keep A's dense metadata
line (period, published date, size) in B's "Earlier documents" list. C's "unopened" idea survives as a quiet
"New" tag driven by the access log, not as a checklist.

IR staff get a separate, table-first console (A's pattern) because they work on desktop with many rows.
